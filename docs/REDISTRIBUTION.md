# Third-party distribution notes

WHAT's LGPL licence covers its own code. Dependencies and company logos retain
their separate terms. Include the relevant notices when distributing a build.

WHAT uses the unmodified Corsair iCUE SDK 4.0.84 binary from the SDK's `redist`
directory. The SDK also includes a general EULA with transfer restrictions;
the scope of redistribution permission is unclear. Refer questions about that
permission to Corsair. Binary releases include the original Overview and
Reference document, including its EULA, as
`licenses/Corsair-SDK-Overview-and-EULA.pdf`.

NVIDIA, HWiNFO and Wallpaper Engine artwork identifies their optional modules.
Brand artwork and trademarks remain subject to their owners' terms. Check their
usage guidance before changing the artwork or using it in promotional material.
Simple Icons artwork uses CC0; trademark rights remain separate.

Component notices and artwork sources are listed in `licenses/THIRD-PARTY.md`,
`licenses/Corsair-SDK.txt` and `assets/brands/README.md` in the source tree.
See [Rebuilding](REBUILDING.md) for library source and replacement instructions.
